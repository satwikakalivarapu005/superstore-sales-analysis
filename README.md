## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes business sales data to help a company understand its performance across products, categories, regions, and time periods.

The goal is to answer key business questions such as:
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which categories or regions are most profitable?
- Where should the business focus to grow faster?

---

## 📁 Dataset

- **Name:** Superstore Sales Dataset
- **Source:** [Kaggle – Superstore Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)
- **Size:** 9,994 rows × 21 columns
- **Data includes:** Orders, Products, Categories, Regions, Sales, Profit, Discounts

## 💡 Key Insights & Recommendations

### 📈 Overall Performance
- **Total Sales:** $2,297,200
- **Total Profit:** $286,397
- **Profit Margin:** ~12.5%
- There is significant room to improve profitability despite strong revenue.

### 🛒 Category Performance
- **Technology** leads in both revenue and profit.
- **Office Supplies** has the highest order volume but moderate revenue.
- **Recommendation:** Increase marketing investment in Technology products.

### ⚠️ Profit Warning — Loss-Making Sub-Categories
- **Tables** and **Bookcases** are operating at a **negative profit**.
- **Recommendation:** Reprice, renegotiate supplier costs, or discontinue these items.

### 🌍 Regional Performance
- **West region** is the strongest market.
- **South region** is the weakest performer.
- **Recommendation:** Investigate South region — boost promotions or reduce costs.

### 📅 Seasonal Trend
- Sales **peak in November–December** (Q4 holiday season).
- **Recommendation:** Plan inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of Q4.

### 🏆 Top Products
- A small set of **top 10 products** drives a large share of total revenue.
- **Recommendation:** Ensure these products are always well-stocked and actively promoted.
